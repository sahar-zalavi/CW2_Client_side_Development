Our Loan Products

Unity Bank offers a range of loan products to support customers with education, business growth, and long‑term financial commitments. Our loan options are designed to assist customers with a variety of financial needs. These loans provide structured borrowing with clear terms, helping customers manage important expenses responsibly.

Student Loans

Student loans support individuals pursuing higher education by helping cover tuition fees, accommodation, and essential living costs. Unity Bank structures these loans with consideration for students’ future financial circumstances.

Eligibility
  • Must be 18 years or older
  • Enrolled in a recognised institution
  • UK residency required
  • Proof of student status

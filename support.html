Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common questions.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Sign in" in the navigation menu, then select "Forgot password". You'll receive an email with instructions to create a new password. For security, password reset links expire after 24 hours.

How do I report a lost or stolen card?

You can instantly freeze your card through the mobile app under Cards → Freeze Card. To report it lost or stolen, call our 24/7 helpline at +44 20 7123 4567 or use the emergency card block feature in online banking.

What are your customer service hours?

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm GMT. For urgent matters like lost cards or suspicious activity, our automated systems and emergency line are available 24/7.

How long do bank transfers take?

Faster Payments: Usually instant, up to 2 hours
BACS transfers: 3 working days
International (SWIFT): 3–5 working days depending on destination

How do I update my contact details?

Log in to online banking and go to Settings → Personal Details. You can update your email, phone number, and address. Some changes may require verification for security purposes.

Is my money protected?

Yes. Unity Bank is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and your eligible deposits are protected up to £85,000 by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).
